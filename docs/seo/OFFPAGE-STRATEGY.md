## Specific pitch concepts, contingent on original evidence

1. Technology publishers: “What a used workstation model name does not tell the buyer.” Offer actual per-unit display/battery/CPU/GPU checks, original photos and a printable checklist. Landing: existing stock-checklist guide.
2. Ecommerce/business publishers: “Why the visible UAE price differs from a final reviewed quote.” Offer dated explicit input/fee/weight assumptions with worked examples; no unpublished customer data. Landing: ordering guide/comparison.
3. Fashion/shopping publishers: foot measurement, exact style-code and size-standard pitfalls, illustrated with original diagrams and official reference links. Landing: sizing guide.
4. UAE Persian media: source/offer comparison and purchase-linked shipping process, without unsupported faster/cheaper promises. Qualify audience fit and conflicts before contact.
5. Genuine suppliers/repair specialists: factual co-authored inspection resource or attribution to original photos/testing. No link swaps at scale or fictitious partnerships.
