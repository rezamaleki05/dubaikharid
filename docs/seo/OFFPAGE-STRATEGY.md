## Specific pitch concepts, contingent on original evidence

1. Technology publishers: “What a used workstation model name does not tell the buyer.” Offer actual per-unit display/battery/CPU/GPU checks, original photos and a printable checklist. Landing: existing stock-checklist guide.
2. Ecommerce/business publishers: “Why the visible UAE price differs from a final reviewed quote.” Offer dated explicit input/fee/weight assumptions with worked examples; no unpublished customer data. Landing: ordering guide/comparison.
3. Fashion/shopping publishers: foot measurement, exact style-code and size-standard pitfalls, illustrated with original diagrams and official reference links. Landing: sizing guide.
4. UAE Persian media: source/offer comparison and purchase-linked shipping process, without unsupported faster/cheaper promises. Qualify audience fit and conflicts before contact.
5. Genuine suppliers/repair specialists: factual co-authored inspection resource or attribution to original photos/testing. No link swaps at scale or fictitious partnerships.

## Outreach workflow and measurement

Before future contact: record target article, editorial fit, current official contact-page URL, proposed asset, independently useful finding, relationship/conflict and whether paid/editorial. Human-review a short personalized pitch. Contact only after a separately authorized outreach phase. Track contacted date, response, earned mention/link URL, rel attribute, target landing, referral sessions and qualified `lead_created`; do not sum WhatsApp clicks as unique customers. Check actual link existence and destination once live, not just a publisher invoice.

Initial operating goal: qualify8 relevant prospects, prepare3 evidence-based pitches and seek2 editorial conversations. These are controllable activity goals, **not guaranteed backlinks**. After28days, judge fit/referral usefulness; do not chase volume with irrelevant domains.

Reject PBNs, guest-post networks, automated backlink packages, hacked/hidden links, comment/forum spam, mass directories, sitewide paid footer links, fake reviews, fake partnerships and paid followed-link schemes.
